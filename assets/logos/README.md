# Logos

The TWO+THREE logo has two parts. The **mark** is a square with a white 2 and 3 stepping up from bottom left to top right. Where they meet, a solid `signal` + fills the gap between them. The **wordmark** is Teko, outlined into shapes and set on two lines, TWO+ over THREE, with its + also in `signal`. The mark is drawn on one 7.85-unit stroke.

- `two-plus-three-lockup.svg`: mark beside the stacked wordmark, the default. Square and letters in `ink` `#151413`, digits in `paper` `#f5f2ea`, both +s in `signal` `#779371`. Use it on `paper` and `paper-raised`.
- `two-plus-three-lockup-reversed.svg`: for dark grounds. Square and letters in dark `ink` `#f2eee5`, digits in dark `paper` `#111110`, +s in dark `signal` `#a3bb9e`.
- `two-plus-three-lockup-mono.svg`: a true one-colour version, ink only with everything else cut through as holes, so it works on any ground: stamps, embossing, foil, kraft paper, single-colour print. The 2 and 3 are cut out, and the + is cut out with a thin keyline of ink around it so it doesn't merge with the digits. Use it at 32px tall or larger; below that the keyline closes up, so use the colour mark.
- `two-plus-three-mark.svg`, `-reversed`, `-mono`: the mark alone, for avatars, book spines and social images. For browser tabs and home screens use the `Favicons` group instead.
- `two-plus-three-wordmark.svg`, `-reversed`: the stacked wordmark alone, for places where the mark already appears nearby.

Rules:
- The two +s are the only colour in the logo, and they're always `signal`. Never recolour them separately.
- If `signal` isn't available, use the mono files. Don't make your own one-colour version.
- Clear space: one quarter of the mark's height on every side.
- Minimum size: mark 20px tall on screen and 8mm in print; lockup 32px tall.
- Don't place the logo on `signal` or `ochre` grounds. Use `paper` or the dark ground.
- The lockup is fixed: don't re-space it or set the wordmark on one line.
- On a separate product's or project's site, the logo sits in the footer as the maker's mark, never in the header. The header belongs to the product's own icon and name.
